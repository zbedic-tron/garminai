Independent project · Early development

Understand your training. Plan your next race.

GarminAI is an independent project exploring how runners can use their own Garmin activity data and evidence-informed training principles to understand progress and prepare for their goals.

Public Garmin integration is not available yet

What we are building

A simple, free-to-use assistant for recreational runners, with an initial opt-in pilot planned with runners from ARK TRON in Serbia.

Activity insights

Understand distance, pace, heart rate, splits, training volume and changes over time.

Training recognition

Identify probable easy runs, long runs, tempo sessions and interval sessions using activity data, not just titles.

Race preparation

Explore evidence-informed guidance for 5 km, 10 km, half-marathon and marathon goals.

Privacy-first design

Our goal is individual authorization, minimum necessary data, and the ability to disconnect and request deletion.

Project status

Not yet accepting Garmin account connections. We have a private prototype and are evaluating the appropriate Garmin Connect Developer Program access for a future multi-user integration. This website is informational; it is not a running data-collection service.

Independent and community-minded

GarminAI is independently developed. ARK TRON is a potential volunteer pilot community, not the operator or owner of this project. GarminAI is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Garmin Ltd. or its affiliates.
