Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026 · Current status: pre-launch development and demonstration.

Important — no live Garmin connection yet. GarminAI Public is under development. Our public website does not currently offer Garmin account authorization, retrieve real users' Garmin activity data, or provide a live training-analysis service. A separate public demonstration MCP server uses fictional exercise records. Descriptions of the future integration below are plans, not claims that these capabilities are currently operational.

1. Who operates GarminAI?

GarminAI is an independent project operated by the maintainer of the zbedic-tron/garminai repository. Privacy-related questions and requests can be initiated through our contact page. Before launch, we will publish a direct private contact method for privacy and data-rights requests; do not include private training or health data in a public GitHub issue.

2. What we collect now

The public project website is an informational GitHub Pages site. We do not currently provide an account registration form, activity-upload form, advertising tracker, or website analytics script. GitHub, as the website hosting provider, may process visitor technical data, including IP addresses and access logs, according to its own policies. If you choose to contact us, we use the information you provide to respond; public GitHub issues can be visible to others.

The separately hosted GarminAI MCP demonstration runs on Render and currently uses only fictional test activities. It is not a live Garmin account integration. Access to the demonstration is restricted by a development credential; the demonstration does not intentionally collect real Garmin account activity records.

3. Garmin data we plan to process after launch

Only after a user voluntarily authorizes the application through Garmin's authorization flow and we complete required security and privacy controls, GarminAI plans to receive permitted activity records through the Garmin Connect Activity API. Depending on the information Garmin makes available and the user's choices, records may include activity identifiers, dates, duration, distance, sport, pace, speed, heart rate, laps or splits, and FIT activity details. Some fitness records can reveal sensitive information or location; we will assess and minimize the data fields used before enabling the live service.

We plan to associate authorized activity records with a user-specific internal identity so that individuals can review their own training. These records will not be treated as anonymous merely because names are omitted. Garmin Starter access does not include historic backfill, so availability of older activities may be limited.

4. Why we plan to use Garmin data

Subject to authorization, GarminAI intends to provide personal training summaries and analysis, including weekly running distance, pace and heart-rate trends, workout-type interpretation, training intensity distribution, changes in workload, and evidence-informed preparation guidance for running events. We do not intend to use Garmin activity records for advertising, data brokerage, unrelated profiling, or sale to third parties.

5. AI Transparency Statement — explicit consent required

Planned AI processing; not yet active. GarminAI intends to use OpenAI-powered conversational analysis to interpret selected user-authorized activity information and produce training explanations and suggestions. This would involve disclosure of the minimum relevant data to the AI service provider for processing. Before any Garmin-derived data is sent to an AI system, we will display a clear, separate explanation and request the user's explicit, affirmative consent. Connecting a Garmin account alone will not be treated as consent to AI processing. Users will be able to withdraw AI-processing consent through an accessible control before this feature launches; withdrawal will stop new AI submissions, subject to any already-completed processing and applicable provider retention. Where AI consent is not given, AI-based analysis will not be available.

GarminAI does not intend to use Garmin-derived user data to train, evaluate, or improve general-purpose AI models. We will verify provider terms, retention options, and Garmin's applicable approval requirements before enabling AI features. We will not transmit Garmin API data directly from Garmin to an unapproved third-party endpoint, and we will not share developer API credentials with third parties.

AI-generated training guidance may be incomplete or inaccurate and is informational, not a medical diagnosis or substitute for professional advice. We will update this statement if the AI provider, purpose, or processing arrangement changes.

6. Service providers and data sharing

Currently used: GitHub Pages (GitHub) hosts the public information website. Render hosts the fictional-data demonstration server; it is not currently used for live Garmin data storage or synchronization.

Planned, subject to review before launch: Garmin provides access to user-authorized activity data; OpenAI may process data deliberately submitted for AI training analysis with explicit user consent. A production infrastructure and database provider have not yet been selected. Their identities, processing purposes, locations and applicable retention arrangements will be disclosed here before real user Garmin data is processed. We will not silently add an undisclosed provider.

Sale: We do not sell Garmin activity data and do not plan to do so. Sharing data with a service provider for a clearly disclosed function is distinct from selling it; where consent or other authorization is required, we will obtain it first.

7. Legal basis, permissions and user choice

The planned service will request only Garmin permissions necessary for its disclosed functions and will follow applicable privacy requirements. Authorization to receive Garmin activity data and separate consent for AI processing will be presented as distinct choices. Users will be able to stop future collection by disconnecting GarminAI or revoking access through the available Garmin account controls. Live connection, consent-management and disconnect features are not yet available; they will be implemented and tested before launch.

8. Access, correction, export, deletion and withdrawal

Before launch, GarminAI will provide a documented way for users to request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of their stored activity information and to revoke consent. Users may also request restriction or objection where applicable. We will provide a private support channel and verify the requester before acting on account data. Because GarminAI currently has no live Garmin user accounts, there are no live Garmin activity records held by this public service to export or delete.

Requests relating to the current website may be raised through the contact page. Do not include secrets or personal activity records in a public GitHub issue. Removing data from GarminAI would not delete the underlying records from Garmin Connect; users must manage their Garmin account separately.

9. Storage, retention and security

Before processing live Garmin activity data, we will finalize the production database, access controls, encryption and secret management, secure deletion process, retention periods, incident response, and account-isolation tests. We will then publish concrete retention periods and deletion timelines here. We will not use Render's temporary free-tier filesystem as a production store for identifiable Garmin activity records. No public Garmin API credentials, OAuth client secrets, or access tokens should be submitted through the informational website or public repository.

10. International processing

GitHub, Render and any eventual AI or infrastructure providers may process data in jurisdictions outside the user's country. Before processing live Garmin data, we will identify relevant provider locations, contractual protections and legal transfer mechanisms, where required, and disclose material details in this notice.

11. Cookies, external links and children

We do not intentionally set tracking cookies on the informational website. External sites may have their own cookies and privacy terms. GarminAI Public is being designed for adult recreational runners; we have not implemented a service for children and will define age restrictions before launch.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy explains the current development stage and planned processing. It does not grant permission to start collecting live Garmin data without the controls and disclosures described above. We will publish a materially revised policy, with an updated effective date and confirmed provider, retention, consent and user-rights details, before launching the live integration.
