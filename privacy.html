Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026 · Scope: this informational GarminAI project website only.

Important: GarminAI is in development. This website does not currently offer public Garmin account connections, accept Garmin API credentials, or collect Garmin activity data.

Who operates this site?

GarminAI is an independent project maintained by the owner of the zbedic-tron/garminai GitHub repository. Contact requests can be made through the project’s contact page.

What data does this site collect?

This is a static informational website. We do not operate a sign-up form, analytics script, advertising tracker, or training-data upload form on it. Website hosting is provided by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and server logs under its own privacy practices.

What happens if you contact us?

If you open a GitHub issue or otherwise contact the project, the information you choose to provide will be used to respond to your inquiry. GitHub issues in a public repository are publicly visible. Do not submit passwords, API keys, health records, detailed training data, or other private information in an issue.

Garmin accounts and fitness information

The public GarminAI integration has not launched. We do not currently request Garmin account credentials or operate a Garmin activity synchronization service for visitors to this website. Before launching any such service, we will publish updated terms and a complete privacy policy describing authorization, categories of data, use, storage, third-party access, retention, security, revocation, and deletion procedures, and will seek any approvals required by Garmin.

Cookies and external services

We do not intentionally set tracking cookies on this website. Links to GitHub and other websites are governed by those services’ own policies.

Your choices

You do not need to provide personal information to browse this site. To ask a privacy question, see the contact page. If you have posted information in a public GitHub issue, you may edit your own contributions or contact the repository maintainer about removal.

Updates

We will revise this policy before the public service begins processing Garmin or other fitness information. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.

Disclaimer

GarminAI is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Garmin Ltd. or its affiliates. Planned training guidance is informational and is not medical advice.
